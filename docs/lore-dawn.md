# Connected Pantheon: Dawn Group

## Ownership And Integration

This work owns only the chronicles for Solarius, Nythea, Aethon, Kairoth, Nashara, and Elaris; the dawn_dream, stars_time, and serpent_archive relationships; and this document. Runtime, loader, catalog, language, and trial files are outside scope. No Gradle builds are used.

The version 2 chronicles overlay the existing catalog. Story titles, branch names and keys, branch deeds, specializations, and reward values remain authoritative in BuiltinNarrativeCatalog.java. Each chronicle follows call/offering, witness, testimony/permanent choice, existing branch deed, reckoning, fresh existing trial, and ending. Witness and reckoning each use one objective shared by both branches; their prose supplies differing interpretations, not different requirements.

Relationships introduce the connection when both gods have been contacted. Their epilogues must remain inaccessible until both stories are complete AND the player explicitly requests the relationship conversation at an appropriate shrine. Completion alone must not reveal an epilogue. Codes aa/ab/ba/bb refer to the gods in each file's listed order. No relationship changes rewards, favor, permanent choices, or independent quest eligibility.

## First Stage: Solarius And Nythea

- Solarius: Dawn's Judgment; a = Judgment, eight undead in daylight, Solar Ward duration 15 seconds; b = Sanctuary, 16 bread, Solar Ward activation below 65% health.
- Nythea: Beneath the Dream; a = Stillness, 1,200 continuous night_safe prayer ticks, nighttime Twilight Veil Night Vision; b = Defiance, four phantoms, nighttime speed bonus 15%.
- Solarius witness: observe/day_sky for 200 consecutive ticks. Ordinary daylight exposes the limits of seeing; shadow is not guilt. Reckoning: harvest six mature crops, connecting either justice or emergency shelter to life after a victory.
- Nythea witness: observe/night_sky for 240 consecutive ticks. Darkness need not be absence, and no special moon is required. Reckoning: plant three saplings at distinct positions, contrasting preservation with the right to develop beyond a keeper's control.
- dawn_dream lists Solarius first and Nythea second. aa is accountable testimony without invented closure; ab is mutual challenge without a retry of history; ba resists confusing survival with complete rescue; bb weighs unconditional welcome against the right to leave.

### Canon, Belief, And Concealment

Canonical backbone: the First Concord protected mortal life from oblivion. Its Sundering forced costly, concealed decisions rather than revealing one secret villain. Solarius sealed the breach believing Nythea had abandoned the defenders. Nythea instead sheltered survivors in dreams and could not return them all. Nashara forged a retreat order to save refugees; Elaris concealed proof; Dravak held the line under the false order; Varun evacuated his pack; Nythea hid these refugees.

Solarius's abandonment accusation is his mistaken belief, explicitly corrected within his independent story. The necessity of sealing does not prove that his entire account was correct. Nythea's assurance that the refugees' sleep would only be temporary is her overconfident concealment of risk, not a canonical guarantee of universal return. Neither account establishes that every missing sleeper died or remains recoverable. The prose never identifies present phantoms with those sleepers.

The cook, shield bearer, dream kitchen, altered song, and angry waking survivor are original remembered vignettes narrated by the gods. They are not discoverable structures, new entities, collectible records, or extra objectives. Their precise details are testimony, not independent proof. The shrine narratives may admit uncertainty without withholding the canonical backbone from players who never contact another god.

## Stars And Time

- Aethon: Reading the Silence; a = Discovery, four ender pearls, Celestial Ward successful-block cooldown 24 seconds; b = Contemplation, 1,200 continuous sky_night prayer ticks above Y=100, successful Celestial Ward block heals one HP.
- Kairoth: The Moment Chosen; a = Patience, 1,200 continuous still prayer ticks, Second Chance restores 60% maximum health; b = Decisiveness, eight hostile kills within 120 seconds of the first qualifying kill, Second Chance grants Absorption I for five seconds.
- Aethon witness: height with target 1, reach Y=160 and return to the shrine. The distant view exposes the people hidden by a large pattern. Reckoning: observe/underground for 240 consecutive ticks below Y=0, requiring attention where celestial sight offers no authority. Height does not substitute for the separate Contemplation prayer deed.
- Kairoth witness: smelt four iron ingots and collect them from a furnace, making sequence and irreversible work concrete without another prayer quota. Reckoning: observe/rain for 200 consecutive ticks while exposed to rain. Patience faces an event it cannot command; Decisiveness learns not to mistake readiness for control. Ordinary rain is sufficient; thunder is not required.
- stars_time lists Aethon first and Kairoth second. aa separates inquiry from implementation; ab pairs prompt warning with explicit costs; ba prevents shared silence from becoming comfortable evasion; bb separates deliberation, intervention, and subsequent scrutiny.

### Canon, Belief, And Concealment

Canonical backbone: these gods are ancient brothers. Aethon foresaw catastrophe. Kairoth withheld a prevention timeline whose price was erasing innocent lives. The content treats it as a rejected possibility, not an erasure Kairoth carried out. Elaris disputes the incomplete chronology. Neither the dispute nor a true prediction establishes an available painless rescue, exhaustive foresight, or a way to replay the Sundering.

Aethon's early belief that the withheld answer would vindicate his anger is explicitly challenged. His prophecy is true but not omniscient. Kairoth's fear that a council would accept the erasure explains his secrecy but remains his prediction about others, not an established fact about how every god would vote. His claim that waiting was neutral is exposed as self-protective interpretation: withholding information was also a decision.

The brothers' annotated chart, the smith's interrupted furnace, and the attendants carrying lamps are original narrated recollections. They establish voice and competing perspectives without adding discoverable relics, alternate dimensions, temporal travel, or new crafting requirements. No chronology is secretly completed by the relationship endings.

## Serpent And Archive

- Nashara: The Serpent's Answer; a = Venom, four poisoned hostile kills, Serpent's Bite poison lasts two seconds longer; b = Subtlety, 600 continuous invisible-and-sneaking ticks near the shrine, successful Serpent's Bite poison applies Weakness I for two seconds.
- Elaris: Two Roads to Wisdom; a = Study, three enchanted books, Foresight permits four dodges per cycle; b = Experience, gain 100 experience points, Foresight cooldown 16 seconds.
- Nashara witness: observe/forest for 240 consecutive ticks, establishing concealment as a context-dependent tool rather than inherent wickedness. Reckoning: breed two animals, moving both paths beyond evasion or lethal efficiency toward a future for the protected. It does not require rare animals or a special settlement.
- Elaris witness: observe/water for 200 consecutive ticks while underwater, with air for a safe return. The changed viewpoint distinguishes a partial reflection from a falsehood. Reckoning: explicitly donate three minecraft:paper through the story action. The modest donation represents separate records of fact, interpretation, and deliberate omission. It requires no written text, document puzzle, collectible evidence, or new item.
- serpent_archive lists Nashara first and Elaris second. aa tests supposedly precise interventions against omitted victims; ab gives lived testimony independence from gratitude; ba separates vulnerable identities from acknowledgment of the forgery; bb allows survivors' needs for secrecy and disclosure to change over time.

### Canon, Belief, And Concealment

Canonical backbone: Nashara and Elaris are former confidants. Nashara forged the retreat order to save refugees, and Elaris concealed proof. Dravak held the line under the false order; Varun evacuated his pack; Nythea hid refugees in dreams. These acts connect the pantheon but are fully explained within each relevant independent chronicle. No contact, offering, testimony retrieval, or favor standing with another god is required.

Nashara's implication that honest appeal certainly would have failed is not objective canon. Her successful rescue does not establish that every deception was necessary, that defenders knowingly accepted her terms, or that rescued people owed her gratitude. Elaris's fear that disclosure would produce reprisals is an explanation for concealment, not proof of inevitable retaliation. Protecting Nashara's reputation and protecting refugee identities are distinct motives. Elaris's own suppression of evidence neither disproves every criticism of Kairoth nor licenses inconsistent standards.

The clerk folding the order, the ungrateful survivor, the archivist asking about an unmarked gap, and the differing household/command-post chronologies are original testimony vignettes. Their purpose is to expose self-serving narration. The three imagined headings are an interpretive exercise, not three physical quest documents. No epilogue creates a tribunal, publishes identifying refugee information, imposes allegiance, removes favor, or awards an additional specialization.

## Player-Facing Constraints

All observations are consecutive ticks; duration text assumes 20 ticks per second. The inspected runtime predicates use open visible sky for day_sky/night_sky, a forest-tagged biome for forest, rain at the player's position for rain, Y below zero for underground, and the player underwater for water. Shrine prayer still uses the existing prayer interaction and location requirements. Narrative descriptions now make the less obvious elevation, exposure, and submersion requirements explicit.

Offering examples were checked against the god source implementations, not inferred from themes. Nashara lists only spider eye and fermented spider eye to avoid potion-content ambiguity. Elaris lists book, written book, and enchanted golden apple; enchanted books remain the unchanged Study donation, not a promise that cursed ordinary offerings are harmless. No branch objective or reward is redefined in the JSON overlay.

The seven playable chapters use call, witness, testimony, deed_a OR deed_b, reckoning_a OR reckoning_b, trial, and ending_a OR ending_b. Both branch passages are authored and counted, but a player reads only the selected path. Trials are the existing god trials and must be newly won after the reckoning. Historical memories do not spawn their described people, places, objects, or events.

## Validation

Read-only Ruby JSON validation checks exact top-level keys and identities, ten exact passage and dialogue keys, paragraph/string types, short chat lines, namespaced identifiers, offering examples, supported objective types, positive integer targets, observation bounds of 100-600 ticks, and the exact aa/ab/ba/bb combinations with the expected ordered gods. It also checks ASCII content and repeated paragraphs. Loader/schema and objective predicate source were inspected for compatibility. No Gradle task, Java compilation, game launch, or runtime integration test was run here; execution of the main agent's loader and gating remains an integration responsibility.

Counts use whitespace-delimited words in premise plus all passage paragraphs, excluding dialogue, objective labels, offering IDs, and documentation. Relationship counts include only each epilogue's paragraphs. These are authored-content totals across both branches, not a promise of 1,200 words on each individual playthrough.
