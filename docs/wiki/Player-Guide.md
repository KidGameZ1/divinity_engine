# Player Guide

## Contact And Blessings

Find or place a god's statue and interact with it to contact that god. Prayer and
accepted offerings build favor according to that god's preferences and the shared
worship budget. You can know multiple gods; progress is tracked independently.

Open the blessings menu to equip eligible blessings. A menu opened by a statue
shows only that statue's god. Normal active slots use rebindable R/G/V defaults;
passives and toggles follow their descriptions. The removed ascension prototype
does not add X/Z/C/B controls.

The Favor panel explains the current relationship and remaining worship rewards.
Budgets reset after server time, not by repeatedly sleeping or logging in.
Large offering stacks cannot bypass the cap.

## Stories And Upgrades

After contact, the god's guide appears in the Codex. Built-in gods use deed-based
relationships: their stories are available after contact, while later blessings
and trial access follow story proofs. Addon gods using the legacy favor track
require at least 50 favor to start a story. Revisit the correct statue and use its story/menu
interaction. If a story prompt appears in chat, close the blessings screen to
use its continuation link. Follow the current journal's exact next action.

The seven chapters are The Call, A Witness, Divided Testimony, The Chosen Deed,
The Reckoning, The Trial and The Return. An accepted offering begins the arc.
Branch confirmation previews both permanent blessing upgrades. Choose carefully:
normal gameplay does not let you switch the permanent branch afterward.

Explicit story donations consume their requested items separately from ordinary
offerings. Only deeds performed during the matching chapter count. Continuous
tasks reset their current attempt when interrupted; completed chapters remain.
Trial failure permits a retry, not a reset of the entire story.

The ending grants 25 favor within the normal cap, lore, shrine recognition and
the selected specialization once. An upgrade works only while its original
blessing is equipped and eligible; it is not a new active slot.

## Trials And Journals

Start trials from the correct statue-opened blessings menu. The server prepares
an isolated protected arena before entry. Logout, death or leaving the arena ends
the trial; cleanup continues in bounded batches. There is no resume-on-login flow.

The Codex keeps original registered guide pages as well as your personal journal,
relationships and discovered lore. Abandonment preserves memories and permanent
choices but pauses that relationship's progression until contact is restored.
Optional paired epilogues give lore, not additional mandatory combat power.

Use the menus and Codex for play. Administrative reset/debug commands are not
required to unlock stories or complete quests.
