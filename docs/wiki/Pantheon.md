# The Pantheon

The active built-in pantheon contains 18 gods and 90 blessings. Their seven-chapter
stories are independently completable; optional paired epilogues reveal different
accounts of the First Concord and its Sundering.

| God | Story | Connected god |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solarius | Dawn's Judgment | Nythea, estranged sun-and-moon sibling |
| Nashara | The Serpent's Answer | Elaris, former confidant |
| Grond | What Endures | Mechanos, former apprentice |
| Varun | The Pack's Claim | Dravak, sworn brother |
| Dravak | The Meaning of Valor | Varun, divided over a retreat |
| Elaris | Two Roads to Wisdom | Nashara, keeper of a compromised archive |
| Voltira | The Storm's Will | Zephra, sibling of the same storm |
| Vokar | The Last Farewell | Terra, old friend across life's boundary |
| Nythea | Beneath the Dream | Solarius, conflicting memories of sanctuary |
| Kairoth | The Moment Chosen | Aethon, ancient brother |
| Cryonis | Winter's Testament | Nerai, sister divided by a drowned sanctuary |
| Nerai | The Water's Promise | Cryonis, preservation versus release |
| Zephra | An Unbound Horizon | Voltira, freedom versus duty |
| Terra | Roots Remember | Vokar, growth and remembrance |
| Lumen | A Light Kept | Ignar, unlikely friend and a damaged refuge |
| Mechanos | The Worth of a Machine | Grond, calculation and accountability |
| Ignar | What the Fire Leaves | Lumen, redemption without erasing harm |
| Aethon | Reading the Silence | Kairoth, foresight and responsibility |

The Codex reveals later chapters and endings through play. This page deliberately
does not expose the unchosen endings. See each discovered god's in-game guide for
accepted offerings, exact objectives, eligibility and reward previews.
